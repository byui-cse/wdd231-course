# Competency Pass-Off

## Purpose

The pass-off is a short meeting with the instructor or TA where you show you can **perform, explain, and adapt** an entire competency. Contracts and peer reviews focus on individual slices. The pass-off is where you bring those slices together.

```text
Contract  →  Peer review (each slice)  →  Instructor/TA pass-off (whole competency)  →  Tracker updated
```

## Before you request a pass-off

Check every item:

- [ ] Every slice of the competency has been reviewed with a peer (see [Peer Contract Review](./contract-review)).
- [ ] Your review records show no unresolved gaps, or you have addressed them.
- [ ] Your work runs and you can show it.
- [ ] You can explain the important decisions from every slice in your own words.
- [ ] You have tests or verification results from across the competency.
- [ ] You can describe at least one challenge and how you solved it.
- [ ] Your [Competency Progress Tracker](../competency-progress-tracker.md) is up to date.

## What to bring

- Links to the contracts and review records for each slice
- The working artifacts, pages, or code
- Tests, screenshots, or notes that verify the results
- One challenge, one success, and one important decision from across the slices

## What happens in the meeting

The instructor or TA may ask you to:

- connect two or more slices in the same workflow,
- explain the relevant code or design,
- demonstrate representative behavior,
- show tests or verification results,
- describe a challenge and its solution,
- make a small change,
- predict what happens in an edge case, or
- diagnose a deliberately introduced small problem.

Questions stay connected to the competency and the slices you reviewed. The goal is not to surprise you with unrelated material.

## Possible results

| Result | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Passed** | You demonstrated and explained the competency and handled the variation. |
| **Pass with next step** | The competency is demonstrated, and there is one small follow-up to complete. |
| **Not yet** | A slice or a connection between slices is missing. Choose the smallest next step and schedule another attempt when ready. |

"Not yet" is useful feedback, not a failure of the course.

## After the meeting

1. Record the result in your Competency Progress Tracker.
2. Write down the next step.
3. Use that evidence to choose your next contract.
